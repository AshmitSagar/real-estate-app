## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal stillness, architectural permanence, and discrete wealth of heritage North Indian luxury hospitality and turnkey estate investments. Built for high-net-worth investors, vacation home seekers, and discerning travelers, the aesthetic avoids ostentatious digital excess in favor of deep atmospheric depth, tactile warmth, and quiet architectural majesty.

The visual direction synthesizes **Modern Architectural Minimalism** with **Warm Editorial Luxury**. Deep espresso, pitch charcoal, and volcanic basalt provide an unyielding foundation, illuminated selectively by brushed champagne leaf, warm amber accents, and creamy alabaster typography. The atmosphere evokes twilight across private Himalayan estates, teakwood pavilions, and intimate courtyard retreats. Interfaces are restrained, generous in breathing room, and structured around stately editorial proportions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered exclusively around an immersive dark theme rooted in natural materials—aged stone, charred teak, raw brass, and woven raw silk.

### Color Roles & Layering
- **Primary (`#C4A47C`)**: Muted champagne gold. Used as the principal accent for focal interactive elements, borders, active states, and key navigational signposts.
- **Secondary (`#211C18`)**: Deep smoked umber. Forms the elevated surface tier for cards, drawer panels, and structural module containers.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Vibrant burnished gold. Reserved for hyper-selective micro-highlights, pristine badges, verified partner insignias, and glowing hover states.
- **Neutral (`#0E0D0B`)**: Deepest black-charcoal obsidian. Serves as the canvas bedrock (`background-body`), guaranteeing maximum visual contrast for estate photography and typography.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Primary Text (`cream-text / #F5F0EB`)**: High-contrast, warm alabaster for headings and primary content.
- **Secondary Text (`sand-muted / #E2DAD1`)**: Warm neutral for body paragraphs, editorial subtitles, and list descriptions.
- **Tertiary Text (`smoke-subtle / #8E867C`)**: De-emphasized metadata, legal disclaimers, and auxiliary captions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes classical aristocratic editorial grace with high-performance operational utility.

- **Headlines & Editorial Accents (`Playfair Display`)**: Brings stately contrast, distinguished letterforms, and warm authority. Headlines feature restrained tracking and generous line heights to preserve breathing room and gravitas.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Inter`)**: Serves as the modern counterweight. Inter delivers exceptional readability at scale across investment figures, specifications, real estate floor plans, and long-form narrative paragraphs.
- **Micro-Labels & Overlines**: Rendered in uppercase `Inter` with wide tracking (`0.18em`) and semi-bold weighting, framing content blocks with the precision of an architectural catalog.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an editorial 12-column grid system paired with deliberate vertical pacing that avoids digital clutter.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 768px`, 4-column layout), Tablet (`768px – 1199px`, 8-column layout), Desktop (`≥ 1200px`, 12-column fixed-max layout capped at 1360px with generous margins).
- **Rhythm**: Sections leverage macro-spacing (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to create an unhurried, gallery-like cadence. Component groupings rely on `space-md` to `space-xl` for disciplined nesting.
- **Asymmetry**: Editorial sections frequently employ alternating column offsets (e.g., 7 columns of photography alongside 5 columns of indented descriptive prose) to reinforce an architectural balance.

## Elevation & Depth

In this dark luxury system, depth is established via subtle lighting gradients, hairline borders, and atmospheric ambient glows rather than standard high-contrast drop shadows.

1. **Surface Stratification**:
   - **Canvas Ground**: `#0E0D0B` (Basalt ground).
   - **Container Level 1**: `#181512` with a 1px border of `#3D3428` (40% opacity).
   - **Container Level 2 / Interactive**: `#211C18` with an edge highlight gradient falling from top-left.
2. **Gold Ambient Luminescence**:
   Elevated interactive elements (such as featured investment cards or hero property highlights) emit a soft, diffused radial glow:
   `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.12), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(223, 202, 160, 0.25)`.
3. **Hairline Precision**:
   Separators and container borders utilize delicate 1px hairline treatments (`#3D3428` to `#6B583E`), simulating hand-cut metallic joinery on dark timber.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft (`1`)** roundedness. Curvatures are understated and architectural, maintaining crisp structural clarity.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem / 4px`)**: Applied to compact interactive components such as buttons, text inputs, chips, and tag pills.
- **Large Corner Radius (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem / 8px`)**: Applied to content cards, modal windows, and hero media wrappers.
- **Extra-Large Corner Radius (`rounded-xl: 0.75rem / 12px`)**: Exclusively reserved for immersive media galleries and full-screen property preview drawers.
- Sharp geometric corners (`0px`) are permitted on full-bleed horizontal dividers and architectural tab bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Solid fill with champagne gold (`#C4A47C`), dark obsidian label (`#0E0D0B`), uppercase typography (`label-button`). On hover, transitions to `#DFCAA0` with a subtle gold glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button**: Transparent background with a crisp 1px border in `#C4A47C`, text in `#F5F0EB`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.08)` and border brightens to `#DFCAA0`.
- **Text Link**: Warm cream text accompanied by a permanent hairline underline in `#C4A47C` spaced 4px below baseline; expands opacity and shifts to champagne on interaction.

### Cards & Investment Modules
- **Property & Service Cards**: Base background in `#181512`, wrapped in a 1px border (`#3D3428`). On hover, the border seamlessly transitions to `#6B583E`, with an upward translation of 4px and a gentle champagne ambient shadow.
- **Image Framing**: Photography maintains a 16:10 or 4:3 architectural ratio with a 0.5rem corner radius, subtly overlaid with a 15% gradient vignetting on the lower third to guarantee text legibility.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- **Input Surfaces**: Background `#12100E`, border 1px `#3D3428`, text `#F5F0EB`, placeholder `#8E867C`.
- **Focus State**: Border transitions to `#C4A47C` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #C4A47C, 0 0 12px rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.2)`).
- **Labels**: Rendered in `label-uppercase` floating above the input in `#DFCAA0`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: 18px square with 3px border-radius. Inactive state: 1px border `#3D3428`, background `#12100E`. Active state: solid `#C4A47C` background with an obsidian checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric circles with active gold core (`#C4A47C`) centered within a dark obsidian ring.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Dark background (`rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.1)`), 1px border (`rgba(196, 164, 124, 0.25)`), text `#DFCAA0` in `label-uppercase`.
- **Metric Badges**: Used for ROI figures, rental yields, and square footage; displayed with prominent numbers in `Playfair Display` paired with compact `Inter` labels beneath.

### Lists & Key Specifications
- Dividers between list items use hairline rules (`#2A241E`).
- Bullet points are replaced with subtle custom gold diamond glyphs (`◆`) or thin brass checkmarks.
